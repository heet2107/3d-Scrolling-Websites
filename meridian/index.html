The rhythm

A day has four hours
that actually matter.

Not twenty-four equal ones. Four windows where your physiology is primed for something specific — and largely deaf to everything else.

05:30 — 09:00

Dawn

Wake the body before you wake the phone.

Light first, then breath, then movement. The first ninety minutes set the slope of everything after them — we spend them deliberately.

11:00 — 14:00

Meridian

Hold the peak without burning it.

Your core temperature, grip strength and alertness top out around now. This is the hour for load, for focus, and for eating like you mean it.

16:30 — 19:30

Dusk

Come down on purpose, not by collapse.

Ojai's pink moment is our cue. Long exhales, slow tissue, warm light, and sound — the practices that tell a nervous system the day is closing.

21:00 — 05:00

Deep

The work you don't feel yourself doing.

Repair happens in the dark. We treat sleep as the primary practice and build the other three hours to protect it.
