## Obligations

- **Public domain / CC0** — no conditions. Credit retained here as a courtesy.
- **CC BY** — attribution required. The table above is that attribution; keep it with the
  images if you redistribute them.
- **CC BY-SA** — attribution **and** share-alike: because the files here are cropped and

  Swap those first if you need a uniformly permissive set.

## What is *not* photography

`assets/art/guide-1..4.svg` are procedural artworks generated by `build/art.py`, not photos.

That is deliberate. The guides are invented people with invented names and biographies, and
attaching those to a photograph of a real, identifiable person would misrepresent them. If
you shoot or commission real portraits of the real people who work at the studio, drop them
in and point the guide slots at them.
