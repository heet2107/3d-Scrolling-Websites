The practice

Eleven people,
one rhythm.

Four guides hold the phases; seven more practitioners cover bodywork, sleep, and the kitchen. Everyone here still practises daily, and everyone still teaches the unglamorous early session.

Est. 2016

Inés Aldana

Founder · Breath

Trained in Buteyko and clinical respiratory physiology, then spent six years unlearning most of the theatre around both. Opened MERIDIAN after a decade of teaching breath in hospitals, where nobody has time for incense.

12 yrs teaching

Tomás Okafor

Movement

Ex-dancer, now a strength coach who programmes for range first and load second. Will happily spend an entire session on your feet, and you will understand why by the end of it.

9 yrs teaching

Neve Lindqvist

Sound & Stillness

Studied composition before she studied practice, which is why her sound sessions have structure instead of drift. Also runs the Deep hour and the sleep clinic.

Chef · RD

Priya Raman

Nourishment

A chef who went back for a nutrition degree because she got tired of arguing with bad advice. Runs the kitchen, writes the seasonal menus, and holds the 1:1 food sessions.
