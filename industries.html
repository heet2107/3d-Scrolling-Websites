Industry versatility

Defense

Tanks, pistons and critical military assemblies produced to stringent defense standards.

Railway

Braking, bogie, coupling and engine components for rolling stock and infrastructure.

Automotive

Forged and machined parts for drivetrain, steering and suspension applications.

Agriculture

Custom projects

One-off and repeat components developed with our tool room and design team.
